Hirvex Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 September 2026

Hirvex is a recruitment and job-search application that helps job seekers discover opportunities and create profiles and applications, and helps recruiters work with job and candidate information.

1. Information You Provide

Depending on the features you use, Hirvex may allow you to enter information such as your name, email address, phone number, location, professional summary, skills, work experience, education, resume information, job details, application information, and cover-letter content. Recruiters may also enter information about their organisation, job postings, and candidates.

2. Photos, Documents and Resume Information

If you choose to select a profile or comment image, or upload a resume, the application may access the file or image you select for the relevant feature. Do not provide information that you do not want included in your profile, application, or other content.

3. How Information Is Used

Information you enter is used to provide the recruitment features you request, including displaying profiles and job information, preparing job applications, showing application details, and supporting recruiter and candidate workflows.

4. Storage and Sharing

The current version of Hirvex uses local device storage for application data and does not represent that information stored locally on your device is transmitted to Hirvex servers unless a feature specifically provides for such transmission. Information that you choose to place in a profile, job posting, application, comment, or other shared feature may be visible to other users as intended by that feature.

5. Network Content and Third-Party Services

Hirvex may load online content, such as video or other resources, from third-party internet services. Those services may process technical information according to their own privacy policies. Hirvex does not control the privacy practices of third-party services.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps appropriate to the current design of the application to protect information handled by Hirvex. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally by the current version of Hirvex remains on your device until it is removed by the application, the application's data is cleared, or the application is uninstalled, subject to the behaviour of your device and operating system.

If you have a question about deletion or believe information associated with Hirvex should be removed, contact us at nikhilkhanna317@gmail.com . We will review and respond to the request.

8. Children's Privacy

Hirvex is intended for job-seeking and recruitment purposes and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children for recruitment purposes.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's features or data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
